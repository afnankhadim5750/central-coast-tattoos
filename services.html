Services

What we do.

Every piece starts with a conversation. Bring an idea, a reference photo, or a blank slate — your artist draws it for your body, and it gets tattooed once it's right.

Custom tattoos

Original one-off designs drawn from your ideas. No flash-off-the-wall repeats — a piece designed for your placement, your size, and your story.

Cover-up work

Regretted ink, faded pieces, and old work reworked into something you're proud to show. Straightforward advice on what's possible before anything starts.

Blackwork & black and grey

Bold linework, heavy black shading, and smooth black-and-grey realism. Work with weight — built to hold its contrast for decades.

Colour tattoos

Traditional and neo-traditional colour with solid saturation — roses, daggers, swallows, skulls, and custom colour pieces that stay bright.

Fine line & illustrative

Delicate linework, illustrative detail, and ornamental designs. Precision pieces for wrists, ankles, behind the ear, and everywhere subtle works.

Walk-ins & flash

Flash designs ready to go when the chairs are free. Follow the studio for flash days — or walk in and ask what's available.

The process

How a piece comes together.

01

Consult

Call the studio or walk in. Talk through your idea, placement, and size with an artist.

02

Design

Your artist draws the piece for your body. You approve it before anything is permanent.

03

Session

Sterile setup, single-use needles, and a steady hand. Big pieces are booked across sessions.

04

Aftercare

You leave with clear aftercare instructions so the tattoo heals clean and stays sharp.

Good to know

Healed right, it lasts.

Every client leaves with aftercare guidance. The short version:

  • Keep the fresh tattoo clean and follow the wrap instructions your artist gives you.
  • Moisturise lightly as it heals — don't pick or scratch the scabbing.
  • Keep it out of the sun, pools, and the ocean until fully healed.
  • Something looks off? Call the studio — they'd rather hear from you early.
