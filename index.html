The studio

A quarter century
of coast ink.

Central Coast Tattoos opened its doors in 2000 — and never closed them. For more than 25 years the studio has been a fixture of the Central Coast, a place where walk-ins become regulars and first-timers become collectors.

Five resident artists, one shared standard: clean lines, solid colour, and work that still looks sharp decades later. The studio holds a 4.9 rating with a 117/120 inspection score — earned the slow way, one session at a time.
